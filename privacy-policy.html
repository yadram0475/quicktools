Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

At QuickTools.in, accessible from our website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors.

1. Data Processing and Storage

All tools on QuickTools.in operate 100% on the client-side (your browser). We do not upload, store, or process any of your personal files, images, or data on our servers. Whatever you do on our tools stays on your device.

2. Google AdSense and Cookies

We may use third-party advertising companies, such as Google AdSense, to serve ads when you visit our website. Google uses cookies (like the DART cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites on the Internet.

3. Contact Us

If you have any questions about our Privacy Policy, feel free to contact us via email at support@quicktools.in.
